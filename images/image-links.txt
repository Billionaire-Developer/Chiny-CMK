NOTE ON THE TEAM SECTION:
The "Meet the Team" section intentionally uses initials in colored circles
instead of stock photos. Using real stock photography with invented names
would misrepresent who actually works for the business to site visitors —
swap in real photos and names once the roster is finalized, not stock images.
